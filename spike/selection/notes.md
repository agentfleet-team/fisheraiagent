## 5. Findings for RFC-3 (written by the builder)

Cloud workflow runs (artifact `spike/selection/results/`): _pending — filled in after the workflow runs on this PR head._

- **Filters bound the window:** across both Mac runs, every week-filter entry that carried age text was <=8 days old and every month-filter entry <=32 days (0 outliers). The upload-date filter is the freshness gate; rank-1 hits are now minutes-to-days old, where the round-1 relevance-sorted search surfaced 46-597-day-old videos at the top.
- **Keyless search from the cloud:** 36/36 yt-dlp search calls on the Mac succeeded; page reads 35/36 (one transient `no ytInitialData in page` on an HTTP-200 body — YouTube occasionally serves a shell page; retry or ignore). Cloud numbers in the table above.
- **Fields without a second call:** at `--playlist-end 20`, flat entries carry `id`, `title`, `duration` every time, and `channel`/`view_count` on most searches; the page read adds the relative age text (604/720 Mac entries) and the CC badge (158/720) — a quality bar can gate on duration, age, views and CC presence with no per-video call. `channel`/`view_count` were absent from yt-dlp's flat entries on 4/36 Mac searches and more often on GHA (section 1 columns) — an A/B layout gap on YouTube's side, not a block; the same values exist in the page's `ownerText`/`viewCountText`.
- **Two fetches, two tails:** the yt-dlp fetch and the page fetch of the same URL disagreed on ~3.5 of 20 ids per search (63 ids each way on mac-1). YouTube rotates tail results between identical keyless requests; entries without `age_text` are mostly these mismatches, not parse failures.
- **Cost of the second call:** ~1.0 s median per search call, ~0.4 s per page read, ~1.3 s per full-metadata call on the Mac. The nightly set (9 queries x 2 filters) costs ~20 s of searches + ~36 s for the top-3 probes.
- **`ytsearchdate` is gone:** the ticket named `ytsearchdate20:`; `YoutubeSearchDateIE` no longer exists in yt-dlp 2026.11.23 (`Unsupported url scheme`). The `/results` URL with an `sp` parameter is the same flat-playlist path.
- **Nightly supply (Mac, deduped over 2 runs, 8-90 min band, page fields only):** <=7 days — 38-47 unique videos per topic; <=30 days — 39-47 per topic. Against round-1's relevance-sorted 0-1/topic (<=7d) and 1-3/topic (<=30d), the corrected search shows the nightly supply is ample, not marginal.
- **Caption presence (Mac, 54 probed):** `zh*` on 49/54, `en` on 47/54, neither on 5/54 (nearly all via `automatic_captions`). The page CC badge is a coarser signal — it marks closed captions exist but not the language; zh/en presence still needs the per-video call.
